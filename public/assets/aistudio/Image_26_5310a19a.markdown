## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a quiet, tactile sanctuary for mobile web interaction. Tailored exclusively for a focused Android browsing experience, the aesthetic blends warm minimalism with literary craftsmanship, counteracting browser clutter, clickbait feeds, and hyper-saturated chrome.

### Brand Character & Emotional Resonance
- **Sanctuary & Clarity:** Interfaces exist purely to serve the content of the web, receding completely when reading or navigating.
- **Literary Grounding:** The touchpoints feel akin to high-grade unbleached paper, fine botanical inks, and tactile editorial tools.
- **Utilitarian Speed:** Lightweight, thumb-first ergonomic reachability with zero decorative friction or superfluous transitions.

### Visual Style
- **Warm Editorial Minimalism:** Anchored by bone-white, unbleached canvas backdrops, paired with deep botanical sage and precise 1px architectural lines.
- **Subtle Organic Tactility:** Micro-surfaces elevate through gentle shifts in warm paper tones rather than heavy synthetic shadows, creating an interface that feels composed, calm, and deliberate.

## Colors

The palette is derived from natural cellulose, fieldstone, and dried botanical pigments. It avoids harsh pitch blacks and clinical blue-whites in favor of warm, low-fatigue tones.

### Palette Roles
- **Canvas Base (`#FAF9F5`):** Soft, warm unbleached paper background applied to root browser viewports and reading canvases.
- **Primary Ink (`#325346`):** Deep forest sage used for active states, key interactive controls, progress indicators, and primary visual anchors.
- **Primary Accent (`#4A6B5D`):** Mid-tone sage for pressed button states, active omnibox highlights, and focused indicators.
- **Secondary Slate (`#526069`):** Grounded slate for secondary navigation iconography, inactive tabs, and technical badges.
- **Muted Sage (`#8C9E94`):** Soft botanical wash for inactive switches, placeholder accents, and light indicator tracks.
- **Primary Text (`#1E2321`):** Deep charcoal olive providing optimal reading contrast without the stark harshness of `#000000`.
- **Secondary Text (`#68706B`):** Subdued field-grey for URLs, timestamps, metadata, and placeholder text.
- **Structural Border (`#E2DDD3`):** Crisp 1px warm sand-grey for container delimiters, address bar borders, and bottom sheet divides.
- **Surface Level 1 (`#F4F2EC`):** Resting surface for top toolbars, bottom action bars, and inset search fields.
- **Surface Level 2 (`#EDEAE1`):** Elevated tone for floating omniboxes, popover sheets, context menus, and modal dialogs.

## Typography

The typographic pairing balances literary editorial grace with strict UI legibility:
- **Newsreader** handles reading mode headlines, history headers, domain identity branding, and immersive text views. Its subtle serif proportions convey craft and scholarly attention.
- **Plus Jakarta Sans** powers all chrome controls, address inputs, menu options, tab counts, and metadata. Its open apertures ensure instantaneous readability at compact mobile scales.

### Hierarchical Usage Rules
- `display-*` and `headline-lg` are reserved for Reader Mode headers, empty-canvas moments, and tab-grid section divisions.
- `body-lg` in Newsreader provides the default setting for distraction-free reader articles.
- `label-*` in Plus Jakarta Sans anchors the bottom navigation bar, context flyouts, security badge chips, and Omnibox URL rendering.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built for direct single-handed thumb interaction within modern Android viewport constraints. 

### Ergonomics & Grid Architecture
- **Thumb-Zone Prioritization:** All primary controls (Omnibox address bar, back/forward, tab switcher, reader toggle) are docked to the bottom safe area. The top edge is preserved solely for status data and uncluttered webview display.
- **Grid Structure:** A 4-column fluid layout on mobile devices (`<600px`), shifting to an 8-column layout for foldables and tablets. 
- **System Offsets:** Outer screen margin is strictly `1rem` (16px), with `0.75rem` (12px) internal grid gutters to allow dual-column tab card views to maximize content density without touching screen bounds.
- **Touch Targets:** Strict adherence to a 48×48dp minimum tappable hit-box for all navigation anchors, even when visual icon footprints are 20dp or 24dp.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual separation relies on warm surface tiering and fine 1px outlines rather than heavy drop shadows.

### Elevation Hierarchy
1. **Level 0 (Canvas Base):** `#FAF9F5` — Flat base layer. No shadow, no border.
2. **Level 1 (Docked Structural Bars):** `#F4F2EC` — Bottom navigation and search bar dock. Delimited by a solid 1px `#E2DDD3` border on intersecting edges.
3. **Level 2 (Floating Floating Cards & Controls):** `#EDEAE1` — Floating bottom Omnibox, active tab tiles, and reader view controls. Supported by a 1px border `#E2DDD3` and a warm, ambient tint shadow: `0 4px 16px -2px rgba(50, 83, 70, 0.06)`.
4. **Level 3 (Overlays & Menus):** `#EDEAE1` — Bottom sheets, context dialogs, bookmark managers. 1px border `#E2DDD3` paired with `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(50, 83, 70, 0.10)`. Scrim overlay uses `#1E2321` with 25% opacity.

## Shapes

The design system uses deliberate, refined corner radii ranging from 8px to 16px (`roundedness: 2`), reflecting an organic, pebble-like quality while preserving space efficiency on compact phone displays.

### Radius Distribution
- **Small Controls (8px / `rounded-md`):** Checkboxes, context menu items, URL security badges, and segmented reader settings.
- **Medium Panels (16px / `rounded-lg`):** Browser tab cards, quick-action dialogs, bottom sheets, and the docked Omnibox input.
- **Pill (9999px / `rounded-full`):** Omnibox interactive chip toggles (e.g., Reader Mode, HTTPS security lock), floating page action pills, and status tags.

## Components

### 1. Bottom Omnibox (Address Bar)
- **Container:** Grounded or floating dock at bottom screen margin. Background `#EDEAE1`, border 1px `#E2DDD3`, radius 16px, shadow `0 4px 16px -2px rgba(50, 83, 70, 0.06)`.
- **Domain Text:** Centered domain name in Plus Jakarta Sans (`label-lg`), `#1E2321`. Trailing path displayed in `#68706B`.
- **Security & Action Badges:** 8px rounded icon button for SSL lock on the left; Reader toggle button (`#325346` when active) on the right.

### 2. Buttons
- **Primary:** Solid `#325346` fill, `#FAF9F5` text, 12px border radius, 44px height. Active/pressed state uses `#4A6B5D`.
- **Secondary / Tonal:** `#F4F2EC` surface, 1px `#E2DDD3` border, `#325346` text. Active state darkens surface to `#EDEAE1`.
- **Icon Actions:** 48×48dp hit-area with 24dp optical SVG icon. Tinted in `#526069`, shifting to `#325346` on press with an 8px circular ink ripple.

### 3. Tab Switcher Cards
- **Inactive Tab Card:** `#F4F2EC` background, 1px `#E2DDD3` border, 12px radius. Webpage preview inset by 6px with an 8px radius. Title rendered in Newsreader (`headline-md`).
- **Active Tab Card:** `#EDEAE1` background, 2px `#325346` border, subtle ambient elevation shadow. Close button anchored top-right with a 32dp tap zone.

### 4. Chips & Security Badges
- **Structure:** Pill shape (`rounded-full`), height 28px, horizontal padding `space-sm` (8px).
- **Default State:** `#F4F2EC` fill, 1px `#E2DDD3` border, `#68706B` text, 14dp icon prefix.
- **Active State:** `#325346` fill, `#FAF9F5` text, borderless.

### 5. Lists & History Rows
- **Layout:** Edge-to-edge `#FAF9F5` or inset `#F4F2EC` list groups.
- **Separators:** 1px solid hairline `#E2DDD3`, indented by 48px to preserve icon vertical alignment.
- **Content:** Title in Plus Jakarta Sans (`body-md`), URL and timestamp in `#68706B` (`body-sm`).

### 6. Reader Mode Viewport
- **Background:** Strictly `#FAF9F5` canvas with zero UI distraction.
- **Article Typography:** Headline in Newsreader `display-lg` (`#1E2321`), body in Newsreader `body-lg` with 1.7 line height for optimal readability.
- **Reading Progress Bar:** 2px slim bar pinned to top edge, colored in `#325346`.

### 7. Form Inputs & Search Fields
- **Container:** Height 44px, surface `#F4F2EC`, border 1px solid `#E2DDD3`, radius 12px.
- **Focus State:** 1px solid `#325346` with no exterior glow ring. Text color `#1E2321`, placeholder `#68706B`.
